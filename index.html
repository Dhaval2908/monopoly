📜 Desi Business Official Rules

📌 Basic Turn Mechanics

Roll the dice to move clockwise around the board. If you roll doubles, you get another turn. Rolling doubles three times in a row sends you directly to Jail.

🏢 Asset Acquisitions & Rent

Landing on an unowned city slot allows you to purchase it for the listed price. If owned, you must pay the owner the designated layout rent. Owning a complete color set allows you to begin building structures to scale up rents.

🛑 Mortgages & Liabilities

If you run low on capital, properties can be mortgaged for 50% of their base value. Mortgaged properties do not collect rent. To unmortgage, you must pay the principal value plus a 10% processing fee.

👮 The Rest House / Jail Loop

While in Jail, you cannot move. You can exit by rolling doubles on your turn, paying a ₹500 fine, or playing a "Get Out of Jail Free" ticket card.
